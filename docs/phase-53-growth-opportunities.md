# Phase 53 — AI Business Growth & Opportunity Engine

Adds a deterministic seller-growth opportunity layer on top of the existing advanced analytics service.

## Coverage

- Profitable SKU scale candidates
- Low-margin recovery opportunities
- Efficient advertising scale candidates
- High-ACOS spend recovery
- Inventory-constrained growth detection
- Overstock turnover opportunities
- Healthy-marketplace scale candidates
- Priority ranking and opportunity summary
- Seller-scoped API: `GET /api/v1/analytics/growth-opportunities`

## Safety

The engine is advisory-only. It does not automatically change prices, budgets, inventory, listings, refunds, or customer communications. Any operational action remains approval-gated through existing automation and command-center workflows.

No competitor facts or marketplace performance are fabricated; recommendations use recorded analytics only.
